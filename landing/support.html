Account Deletion Request

We respect your right to control your personal data. If you wish to delete your Digital Coffee account and all associated data, you can do so easily.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. In-App Deletion (Recommended):
    • Open the Digital Coffee app
    • Go to Settings → Account Settings
    • Select "Delete Account"
    • Follow the confirmation prompts
  2. Email Request: Send an email to our support team using the contact information below with the subject line "Account Deletion Request"

What happens when you delete your account:

  • Your account will be permanently deleted within 30 days
  • All personal data (name, email, profile information) will be removed
  • Your wellness data (mood tracking, journal entries, meditation history) will be deleted
  • Your coaching session history and notes will be permanently removed
  • Active subscriptions will be cancelled (no refund for unused time)
  • This action cannot be undone

Data Export Request

Before deleting your account, you may want to export your data. We can provide you with a copy of your personal information in a structured, machine-readable format.

To request a data export, email us at the address below with the subject line "Data Export Request". We will process your request within 30 days.

Data Retention

After account deletion, we retain minimal data only as required by law or for legitimate business purposes:

  • Transaction records for tax and accounting purposes (up to 7 years)
  • Fraud prevention and security logs (up to 2 years)
  • Aggregated, anonymized analytics data (indefinitely)

All other personal and identifiable data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of your deletion request.

Other Privacy Requests

You have the right to request:

  • Access: A copy of all personal data we have about you
  • Correction: Updates to inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Restriction: Limitation of how we process your data
  • Portability: Transfer of your data to another service
  • Objection: Stop processing your data for certain purposes

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below.
